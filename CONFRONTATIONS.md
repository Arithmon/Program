scheduled experimental data. Charter rule 2 applies: no prediction is revised
after the fact; verdicts are recorded favorable or not.

## Verdict log

Dated entries, appended as data arrives. Never edited, only appended.

- **2025 (NuFIT 6.0).** delta_CP best fit moved to 177 degrees; the frozen
  197-degree prediction sat outside the 1-sigma range. Recorded as
  unfavorable at the time.
- **Late 2025 (NuFIT 6.1).** delta_CP best fit moved to 207 to 212 degrees;
  the unchanged 197-degree prediction returned to within about 1 sigma. The
  prediction did not move between these two entries; the data did. In the
  same release, the theta23 best fit flipped to the lower octant, putting the
  upper-octant bet in tension. Both verdicts recorded.
- **2026-11-27 (framework v3.6).** The table above is restated as version 3.6
  of the framework paper states it ([10.5281/zenodo.24584099](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24584099),
  main section 11). delta_CP stays at 197 degrees. Two statements of this page
  change, and the change is recorded here as a change of the stated bet, not
  as a verdict. First, the "operational window 182 to 212" for delta_CP is
  withdrawn: the rule is rejection when a global fit excludes 197 degrees at
  3 sigma, with no window. Second, version 3.5 carried two expressions for
  theta23: arcsin(25/33) = 49.25 degrees, which this page showed, and
  sin^2(theta23) = 6/11 (about 47.6 degrees), the relation of its dataset.
  Version 3.6 keeps the dataset relation only; both lie in the second octant.
  The sin^2(theta12) = 4/13 row is added, with JUNO as its named decider. Version 3.6 also reports that six of the 33 relations are
  excluded as exact equalities at more than 3 sigma and are kept as numerical
  proximities; they are listed in the paper (main section 4) and are not open
  bets.

## Calendar notes

- Global neutrino fits (NuFIT) have historically been released every one to
  two years; each release appends a verdict entry here.
- Hyper-Kamiokande and DUNE are the named deciders for both neutrino bets;
  first decisive oscillation datasets are expected from the late 2020s
  (Hyper-K) and the early 2030s (DUNE).
