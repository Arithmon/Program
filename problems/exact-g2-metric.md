**Statement.** Promote the explicit Donaldson-type near-solution on K7 (K3
fibration over S3, Z2^3 fiber symmetry) to an exact torsion-free G2 metric,
or characterize the obstruction.

**Why it matters.** This is the program's central lock. The near-solution
is explicit; exactness is what separates "a candidate" from "the geometry". Correction (K7 erratum,
2026-08-22): the closed-form residual bound of the v3.5 release certifies an
auxiliary quantity, not the Calabi-Yau residual; in the correct pullback
convention the residual variance of that ansatz is 5.3e-1, not small.
Correction (K7 erratum §9, 2026-10-22): the Joyce-Karigiannis route of the
v3.5 release does not realize (b2, b3) = (21, 77); no construction of a
compact G2 manifold with these Betti numbers is currently known.

**Known constraints.** Existence of a compact G2 manifold with (21, 77) is
itself open, and a closed-form exact representative is research-level. Blockwise Newton-Kantorovich contractibility near the
near-solution has been verified on all 7 nontrivial Z2^3 isotypic blocks
(margins 7x). Four classical construction templates (Joyce, TCS, extra-twisted
TCS, CCY7) are excluded by theorem; the object is not covered by existing
taxonomies.

**Next step.** Full adiabatic coupling across blocks (Stage 2 of the
Kantorovich program); singular Monge-Ampere and continuity-method routes
scoped as alternatives.
